# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical recruiters, hiring managers, and small tech companies evaluating Maurizio Compagnone for junior web developer roles. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, or a direct link, usually on desktop, and need to understand in a few minutes what he can build, how he works, and how to contact him. A secondary audience is developers who open the linked repositories and releases to verify the work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Maurizio Compagnone, a junior web developer based in Castelfiorentino, Italy. It presents real projects, professional experience, education, and contact details. Success means a recruiter can quickly see evidence of shipped software, understand the profile, and start a conversation without asking for information already on the page.

## Positioning

The portfolio shows end-to-end ownership: web interfaces, desktop applications, and a native Android app, each carried from implementation to packaging, distribution, or a working preview. The claim is not a list of technologies; it is that the projects are real, runnable, and documented, including their limits.

## Operating Context

The site is read during hiring evaluation, often in a quick scan before a longer read. It supports Italian and English with equivalent content. Project cards link to public repositories and releases where they exist. The contact form is the main conversion path. The site is deployed as a Next.js application and is not a commercial service catalog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP/Framer Motion, and EmailJS for the contact form.
- Italian and English dictionaries with matching structure.
- No accounts, no backend dashboard, no e-commerce, and no commercial service pages.
- No personal data beyond email, GitHub, and LinkedIn.
- Projects must be real and verifiable. Do not invent metrics, testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or release claims.
- Audit Mobile is a 1.0 preview with no public download link; the card must not imply a public store release.
- The portfolio itself is one of the showcased projects and links to its public repository.

## Product Principles

1. Show shipped work before describing skills.
2. Explain the problem each project solves, not its visual style.
3. State status and limits honestly; a preview is a preview.
4. Keep the page fast to scan on desktop and phone.
5. Keep Italian and English in parity, with no content that exists in only one language.
